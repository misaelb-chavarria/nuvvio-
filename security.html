Information Security & Regulatory Compliance

A detailed overview of Nuvvio’s defense-in-depth security architecture, strict data sovereignty governance, and cryptographic frameworks designed to protect highly sensitive marine geospatial intelligence and institutional digital twins.

1. Cryptographic Standards & Data Protection

Because Nuvvio processes sub-meter resolution bathymetry and critical infrastructure routing grids, our cryptographic posture aligns with defense-grade requirements. We enforce strict data-at-rest and data-in-transit encryption protocols across all distributed compute nodes and storage clusters.

Data at Rest

All persistent geospatial volumes, relational databases, and AI model weights are encrypted using AES-256-GCM. Master encryption keys are rotated automatically every 30 days and managed via FIPS 140-2 Level 3 validated Hardware Security Modules (HSMs).

Data in Transit

Internal microservices communication and external API payloads are secured exclusively via TLS 1.3. Legacy protocols (TLS 1.1/1.2) are strictly deprecated to prevent downgrade attacks and ensure Perfect Forward Secrecy (PFS).

2. Data Sovereignty & Geo-Fencing

Marine intelligence often intersects with national security, particularly when processing telemetry within a nation's Exclusive Economic Zone (EEZ). Nuvvio respects the sovereign limits defined by the United Nations Convention on the Law of the Sea (UNCLOS).

To comply with stringent localized data residency laws, Nuvvio employs dynamic geo-fencing and tenant isolation. Enterprise and governmental clients can mandate that all ingestion, inference, and storage of their specific regional data occurs exclusively on physically isolated cloud availability zones within their respective national borders. Cross-border replication of classified bathymetric layers is programmatically blocked at the network routing level.

3. Zero-Trust Architecture & IAM

Nuvvio operates on a strict Zero-Trust network topology. We assume that no entity—internal or external—is implicitly trusted. All requests to access geospatial rendering endpoints, digital twin physics simulators, or administrative consoles are continuously verified.

  • Role-Based Access Control (RBAC): Granular IAM policies restrict data access down to the spatial bounding-box level. Users only access the specific maritime sectors they are authorized to query.
  • Ephemeral Compute Nodes: AI bathymetric inversion workloads are processed on ephemeral, stateless container clusters. Once inference is complete, the compute node is destroyed, leaving no residual memory dumps or cache artifacts.
  • Identity Federation: Full support for enterprise Single Sign-On (SSO) via SAML 2.0 and OpenID Connect (OIDC), enforcing strict Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) policies across all institutional accounts.

4. Regulatory Compliance & Export Controls

Given the dual-use nature of high-resolution satellite observation and bathymetric inversion technologies, Nuvvio strictly adheres to global regulatory and export control frameworks.

  • ISO/IEC 27001:2022: Our core Information Security Management System (ISMS) governing software development, AI model training, and API delivery is independently audited and aligned with ISO 27001 standards.
  • SOC 2 Type II: Nuvvio maintains continuous compliance monitoring to validate the security, availability, and processing integrity of our marine intelligence platform.
  • ITAR & EAR Considerations: Certain ultra-high-resolution subsea intelligence outputs may be subject to the U.S. Export Administration Regulations (EAR) or similar international export controls. Nuvvio implements automated screening and end-user verification protocols to ensure compliance with international non-proliferation and defense guidelines.

Privacy Policy & Legal Terms of Processing

A legally binding disclosure detailing the collection, processing, transfer, and protection of personal and telemetry data under the Federal Law on Protection of Personal Data Held by Private Parties (LFPDPPP), GDPR compliance frameworks, and corporate risk mitigation protocols.

1. Identity and Corporate Domicile of the Responsible Party

In strict compliance with the provisions set forth in the Federal Law on Protection of Personal Data Held by Private Parties (Ley Federal de Protección de Datos Personales en Posesión de los Particulares - LFPDPPP), its accompanying Regulations, and applicable international data protection standards (including the General Data Protection Regulation - GDPR where applicable), Aethel Neural Labs, S.A.P.I. de C.V. (hereinafter referred to as "the Responsible Party", "Aethel", or "We"), acts as the data controller responsible for the collection, handling, storage, use, transfer, and ultimate deletion of your personal data.

For all legal intents, notices, and communications regarding the exercise of privacy rights or data governance inquiries, our official corporate domicile is established within the Mexican United States. Data privacy inquiries, ARCO requests (Access, Rectification, Cancellation, and Opposition), and compliance audits must be formally addressed to our designated Data Protection Officer (DPO) via electronic mail at: legal@aethelneural.com.

2. Categories and Classification of Collected Data

To execute our core neural intelligence operations, telemetry processing, API integrations, and enterprise platform services, Aethel collects distinct tiers of data depending on user interaction models. We strictly limit collection to data necessary for fulfilling explicit commercial, computational, and regulatory obligations:

  • Identification and Contact Data: Full legal name, corporate affiliation, institutional designation, professional electronic mail addresses, direct telephone contacts, and encrypted billing telemetry.
  • Technical and Telemetry Data: IP addresses, browser fingerprints, API access tokens, hardware identification strings, session logs, cryptographic signatures, and execution timestamps associated with platform interaction.
  • Derived Neural and Analytical Data: Parametric configurations, model optimization parameters, structural telemetry matrices, and proprietary computational inputs processed through our neural processing pipelines. We expressly do not collect sensitive personal data (datos personales sensibles) as defined under Article 3, Fraction VI of the LFPDPPP unless explicitly mandated by specialized contractual frameworks with heightened security clearances.

3. Primary and Secondary Purposes of Data Processing

The personal and technical data gathered by Aethel Neural Labs, S.A.P.I. de C.V. is processed under strict operational necessity, contractual execution, and legitimate business interest frameworks, categorized into primary and secondary purposes:

Primary Purposes (Necessary for Legal and Contractual Existence)

Data utilization is strictly bound to: (i) Provision, maintenance, scaling, and security auditing of Aethel’s neural intelligence and telemetry architectures; (ii) Execution and enforcement of software licensing, enterprise agreements, and Terms of Service contracts; (iii) Authentication of API endpoints, prevention of unauthorized system intrusions, and mitigation of cybersecurity vectors; (iv) Fulfillment of statutory tax, accounting, judicial, and regulatory mandates imposed by Mexican and international jurisdictions.

Secondary Purposes (Optional Commercial Optimization)

Subject to explicit user consent or opt-out mechanisms, data may be utilized for: (i) Dissemination of technical whitepapers, research briefings, platform updates, and technological newsletters; (ii) Internal aggregative analytics, neural network training optimization, and machine learning parameter refinement using anonymized or pseudonymous telemetry vectors. Refusal to consent to secondary processing shall under no circumstances impede the delivery of primary contractual services.

4. Mechanisms for ARCO Rights Exercise

In accordance with Chapter II of the LFPDPPP, data subjects hold permanent rights regarding their personal data: Access to personal records, Rectification of inaccurate data, Cancellation upon statutory expiration or unlawful processing, and Opposition to specific processing activities.

To exercise ARCO rights, data subjects must submit a formal written request to legal@aethelneural.com containing: (1) Full legal name and verified electronic mail address; (2) Documents substantiating legal identity (official government-issued identification or corporate power of attorney); (3) A clear, unambiguous description of the specific personal data concerning which rights are exercised; and (4) Any supplementary element facilitating data location. Aethel shall issue a determination within a maximum timeframe of twenty (20) business days from receipt, executing valid requests within fifteen (15) subsequent business days.

5. Data Transfers, National and International Disclosure

Aethel Neural Labs, S.A.P.I. de C.V. implements stringent non-disclosure and data minimization policies. Personal data is never sold, leased, or commercially traded to third parties. Data transfers are strictly executed pursuant to Article 37 of the LFPDPPP under the following narrow exceptions:

  • Subsidiaries and Affiliated Entities: Transfer to corporate affiliates, holding entities, or technical infrastructure partners operating under identical internal data governance standards and binding corporate rules.
  • Cloud Infrastructure & Processing Partners: Disclosure to certified cloud computing, encryption, and neural processing vendors bound by strict data processing agreements (DPAs) requiring equivalent security controls.
  • Legal and Judicial Compliance: Mandatory disclosure to competent administrative authorities, judicial tribunals, or regulatory bodies pursuant to a legally binding subpoena, court order, or statutory mandate.

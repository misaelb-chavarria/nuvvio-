Partnerships & Telemetry MAY 18, 2026

Selixor Technologies Announces Integration of Sentinel-3 OLCI & SWOT Altimetry Ingestion Pipelines

Nuvvio has finalized the integration of high-frequency telemetry streams from Copernicus Sentinel-3 OLCI and NASA/CNES Surface Water and Ocean Topography (SWOT) satellite constellations into its core inversion engine. This milestone expands Nuvvio's capability to process macro-scale sea surface height anomalies and coastal turbidity dynamics at unprecedented temporal frequencies.

The updated ingestion pipeline allows maritime operators to receive updated nearshore hydrodynamic and bathymetric risk vectors every 48 hours, significantly accelerating survey cycles for coastal defense and port management entities.

Industry Alliances MARCH 04, 2026

Nuvvio Collaborates with European Offshore Wind Consortia for Seabed Integrity Monitoring

Nuvvio has entered into a strategic collaboration agreement with leading North Sea offshore energy developers to provide subsea digital twin monitoring for submarine power cables and offshore wind turbine foundation zones. By combining multi-spectral optical inversion with hydrodynamic shear stress modeling, Nuvvio will monitor benthic sandwave mobility around critical subsea infrastructure assets.

This initiative aims to optimize subsea cable burial depths and drastically reduce the requirement for capital-intensive ROV (Remotely Operated Vehicle) sea trials during preliminary planning phases.

Research & Development JANUARY 12, 2026

Publication of Physics-Informed Neural Network Framework for Deep Water Column Radiative Transfer

Research engineers at Selixor Technologies have released a benchmark technical whitepaper detailing the mathematical formulations behind Nuvvio's Physics-Informed Neural Networks (PINNs). The paper addresses long-standing challenges in optical bathymetry, specifically decoupling sea-bottom albedo from suspended particulate matter in optically complex coastal waters.

The full methodology and peer-reviewed benchmark datasets are now accessible through Nuvvio's Academic Access portal for verified research institutions.
